steps:
  - If using fresh tomatillos and chiles, boil or roast the tomatillos until soft, and char/roast the chiles until blackened, then peel.
  - Blend the tomatillos, chiles, scallions, cilantro, garlic, jalapeño (if using), lime juice, broth, and cumin until smooth.
  - Heat the oil in a saucepan, pour in the blended mixture, and bring to a simmer.
  - text: Simmer 8–10 minutes to meld the flavors and reduce slightly.
    timer_minutes: 9
  - Reduce heat to low, add the cheese, and stir until melted and smooth.
  - Season with salt to taste. Serve warm over enchiladas, or with tortilla chips.
notes:
  - "This is a fan-made copycat, synthesized from several independent online attempts to recreate Chuy's Boom-Boom sauce — not their actual proprietary recipe."
  - American cheese is what several sources point to as the trick to that smooth, glossy Chuy's texture — Monterey Jack or pepper jack work too, but may not melt quite as silkily.
  - Hatch chiles are the traditional choice, but Anaheim or poblano work fine too. Adjust the jalapeño/serrano for more or less heat.
  - Pairs directly with the Chicka-Chicka Boom-Boom Enchiladas recipe on this site.
