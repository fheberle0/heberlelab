steps:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté (normal). Add the olive oil and 1 tbsp butter. Once melted, stir in the garlic and lemon zest, and sauté 30 seconds. Add the rice and stir to coat, 1–2 minutes.
  - Pour in the 1¾ cups broth, lemon juice, salt, and honey (if using). Stir once to combine, scraping up any browned bits.
  - Place a trivet directly over the rice. Arrange the salmon fillets on the trivet, season lightly, and dot with the remaining butter.
  - text: Lock the lid, set the valve to sealing, and cook on high pressure for 3 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release the rest. (Add 1 minute for thick fillets; subtract 1 minute for thin ones.)
    timer_minutes: 3
  - Carefully remove the salmon. Fluff the rice with a fork, adjust seasoning, and drizzle with a little of the lemon-butter broth. Serve the salmon over rice, garnished with parsley or dill.
notes:
  - "Optional creamy variation: after removing the salmon, stir 2 tbsp heavy cream or 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp Parmesan into the rice before serving."
  - Line the trivet with parchment or foil for easy cleanup.
  - Add broccoli or peas right after releasing pressure, then cover 2 minutes to steam.
  - "For brown rice: use 2 cups liquid and cook 9 minutes with a 10-minute natural release."
