steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a 1.5-quart (about 1.4L) baking dish.
  - Melt the butter in a small saucepan and stir in the flour. Whisk in the milk slowly, and season with salt and cayenne.
  - Cook the sauce over low heat until smooth and boiling, stirring occasionally.
  - Reduce heat and stir in 1 cup of the cheddar.
  - Layer half the sliced potatoes in the baking dish, then pour half the cheese sauce over them. Repeat with the remaining potatoes and sauce.
  - Sprinkle the remaining cheddar on top, and dust with paprika.
  - text: Bake uncovered about 1 hour, or until the potatoes are fork-tender.
    timer_minutes: 60
notes:
  - Slice the potatoes thin — this is the single most important step for good texture.
  - Yukon Gold and Russet potatoes both work well here.
