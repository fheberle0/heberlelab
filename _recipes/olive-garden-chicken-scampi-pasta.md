steps:
  - Melt 2 tbsp butter in a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat and cook the bell peppers and onion 2–3 minutes until slightly softened. Remove and set aside.
  - Add the remaining 2 tbsp butter to the pan. Once melted, whisk in 2 tbsp of the flour and the garlic, cooking about 20 seconds.
  - text: Add the red pepper flakes, oregano, basil, wine, and chicken broth. Let reduce over medium heat about 18–20 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 19
  - While the sauce reduces, cook the pasta one minute short of the package instructions. Do not rinse.
  - Heat the olive oil in a second pan over medium-high heat. Coat the chicken tenderloins in salt and pepper, then toss in the remaining flour. Cook 3–4 minutes per side, then remove from heat.
  - Once the sauce has reduced by half, whisk in the heavy cream until thickened. Stir in the lemon juice and half the parmesan.
  - Toss the pasta with the sauce, bell peppers, and onion. Top with the chicken and remaining parmesan, and serve.
