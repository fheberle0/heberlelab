steps:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté. Stir together the salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika, and rub all over the roast.
  - Drizzle oil in the pot, wait 30 seconds, then sear the roast 3–4 minutes per side until well browned on all sides.
  - text: Switch to pressure cook on high for 60–80 minutes (60 for a 1.4 kg / 3 lb roast, 80 for a 2.3 kg / 5 lb roast). Arrange the potatoes, onion, and carrots around the roast, then pour the beef broth and Worcestershire sauce over everything. Seal the lid.
    timer_minutes: 70
  - Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then vent the remaining steam before removing the lid.
  - Transfer the roast, potatoes, onions, and carrots to a platter and shred the roast with two forks. Strain the broth. Set the pot to the soup/sauté setting, whisk the cold water and cornstarch together, and stir into the boiling broth until thickened into a gravy. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder to taste.
  - Serve the gravy over the roast and vegetables, garnished with fresh thyme or parsley if desired.
notes:
  - For baby carrots instead of chunks, shorten the pressure-cook time by 10 minutes and add them separately for a final 10-minute pressure cook, so they don't turn to mush.
  - "From frozen: add 20–30 minutes to the pressure-cook time, and you can skip searing — just rub the spices on and go straight to pressure cooking."
