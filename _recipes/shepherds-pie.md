steps:
  - section: Meat filling
    text: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high for 2 minutes. Add the onion and cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  - section: Meat filling
    text: Add the beef or lamb and break it apart with a wooden spoon. Add the parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned.
  - section: Meat filling
    text: Add the Worcestershire sauce and garlic, stir to combine, and cook 1 minute.
  - section: Meat filling
    text: Add the flour and tomato paste and stir until no clumps remain.
  - section: Meat filling
    text: Add the broth, frozen peas and carrots, and corn. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Set the meat mixture aside and preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
  - section: Potato topping
    text: Cover the potatoes with water in a large pot, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook until fork-tender, 10–15 minutes.
  - section: Potato topping
    text: Drain and return the potatoes to the hot pot for 1 minute to evaporate any remaining liquid.
  - section: Potato topping
    text: Add the butter, half and half, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Mash and stir until combined, then stir in the parmesan.
  - section: Assemble
    text: Spread the meat mixture in an even layer in a 23x23cm (9x9-inch) or similar baking dish. Spoon the mashed potatoes on top and spread evenly.
  - section: Assemble
    text: If the dish looks very full, place it on a rimmed baking sheet to catch any bubble-over.
  - section: Assemble
    text: Bake uncovered for 25–30 minutes. Cool 15 minutes before serving.
    timer_minutes: 28
