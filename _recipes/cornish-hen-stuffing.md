steps:
  - section: Stuffing
    text: Melt the butter in a large skillet. Add the onion, celery, and a big pinch of salt, and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
  - section: Stuffing
    text: Combine the bread, onion mixture, and salt in a large bowl. Mix until the bread is moistened.
  - section: Hen
    text: Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Have a small roasting pan or 8x8-inch dish ready.
  - section: Hen
    text: Mix the thyme, salt, and pepper together in a small bowl.
  - section: Hen
    text: Fill the hen with stuffing. Add any leftover stuffing to the bottom of the pan, or use a roasting rack if there's none left over.
  - section: Hen
    text: Tie the hen's legs together with oven-safe twine (optional, keeps the legs in place), then place on the stuffing or rack.
  - section: Hen
    text: Brush the hen with olive oil, then rub the thyme mixture all over, working it under the wings, legs, and skin where possible.
  - section: Hen
    text: Bake 50–60 minutes, or until the juices run clear when the thickest part is pierced and the internal temperature reaches 82°C (180°F).
