steps:
  - Sprinkle the onion on the bottom of a 6–8 quart slow cooker, then lay the chicken thighs on top. Add the pepperoncini, garlic, and butter, then pour in the stock, pepperoncini liquid, and soy sauce. Stir to combine.
  - Cover and cook on low for 6 hours.
  - Once the chicken is done, pull it apart with two forks. Stir the herbs into the gravy. Taste for salt and serve hot.
notes:
  - "Sibling to Mississippi pot roast — same pepperoncini-and-butter backbone, but soy sauce and fresh herbs stand in for packaged ranch and au jus seasoning."
  - Good with mashed potatoes, rice, or greens to soak up the gravy; peas add a nice sweetness on the side.
  - Leftovers keep in the fridge up to 3 days, or freeze up to 3 months.
