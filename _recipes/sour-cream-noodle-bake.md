steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F).
  - Brown the ground chuck in a large skillet. Drain the fat, then add the tomato sauce, salt, and plenty of black pepper. Simmer while you prepare the other ingredients.
  - Cook the egg noodles until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  - Combine the sour cream and cottage cheese in a medium bowl with plenty of black pepper and a pinch of red pepper flakes. Stir into the noodles, then add the green onions.
  - Layer half the noodles in a baking dish, top with half the meat mixture, then half the Cheddar. Repeat with the remaining noodles, meat, and a final layer of cheese.
  - text: Bake until all the cheese is melted, about 20 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - Serve with crusty bread.
notes:
  - "To freeze: assemble in a disposable oven-proof pan, seal tightly, and freeze."
  - "To cook from frozen: bake covered at 190°C (375°F) for 45 minutes, then uncover and bake until lightly browned and bubbly, about 20 more minutes."
