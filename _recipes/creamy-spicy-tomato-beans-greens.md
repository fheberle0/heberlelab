steps:
  - text: Heat 2 tbsp oil over medium in a medium skillet. Stir in the panko, season with salt and pepper, and cook, stirring frequently, until toasted and golden, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a paper-towel-lined plate and wipe out the skillet.
    timer_minutes: 3
  - Heat another 2 tbsp oil in the skillet over medium. Add the onion, garlic, and crushed red pepper, season, and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, about 4 minutes.
  - Add the tomato paste and stir until darkened and combined, about 3 minutes.
  - text: Stir in the beans, cream, sun-dried tomatoes, and 1/3 cup water. Simmer, stirring occasionally, until flavors meld, about 5 minutes. Stir in half the cheese and season to taste.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - Toss the arugula with the seasoned panko, lemon zest and juice, the remaining cheese, and the remaining 2 tbsp oil in a medium bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Pile the greens at the center of the bean mixture. Serve with toasted bread if desired.
