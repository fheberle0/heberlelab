steps:
  - Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a freezer bag or glass bowl. Add the chicken and turn to coat. Marinate 30 minutes at room temperature, or refrigerate up to 12 hours (longer is better, up to 4 hours is a good minimum).
  - Remove the chicken from the fridge and let it rest 15–30 minutes before cooking.
  - "To grill: preheat to 200°C (400°F), clean and grease the grates."
  - text: Grill undisturbed for 5–7 minutes per side, until the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F). Let rest 5 minutes before slicing.
    timer_minutes: 6
notes:
  - "Stovetop alternative: sear in 1 tbsp oil in a hot skillet, 3–4 minutes undisturbed, flip, cover, reduce heat to medium, and cook 5–7 more minutes to 74°C (165°F)."
  - "Oven alternative: bake at 220°C (425°F) uncovered for 16–18 minutes (thickness-dependent) to 74°C (165°F)."
  - Works with any cut — thighs, tenderloins, drumsticks, wings — just adjust cooking time and check with a thermometer.
  - Pound chicken to even thickness first so it cooks evenly; slice very thick breasts into cutlets before marinating.
  - Don't marinate longer than about 12 hours — the acid can start to toughen the texture.
