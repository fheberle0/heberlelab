steps:
  - Combine the bread crumbs, yogurt, garlic, scallions, oregano, crushed red pepper, and 1 tsp salt in a large bowl. Add the chicken and gently mix until fully combined.
  - Coat your palms in olive oil, then shape the mixture into 14 medium meatballs (about 2 inches each), re-oiling your hands as needed. Set on a sheet pan or parchment.
  - text: Heat a 12-inch Dutch oven over medium for 2 minutes until hot. Add 2 tbsp olive oil, tilting to coat, then add the meatballs. Cook, turning occasionally as they release, until golden halfway up the sides, about 7 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 7
  - Meanwhile, toss the chard, lemon slices, cumin, and remaining 2 tbsp olive oil in a large bowl, seasoning with salt and scrunching the leaves as needed.
  - text: Smother the meatballs with the greens and lemon slices. Cover, reduce heat to medium, and cook until the meatballs are cooked through and the greens are tender, 12–13 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 13
  - Use a soup ladle to scoop meatballs onto the greens, browned side up, spooning the pan juices over everything. Cut the remaining lemon half into wedges for serving.
notes:
  - The greens steam over the meatballs rather than around them — an easy way to cook the whole meal in one pot.
