steps:
  - Bring a pot of water to a boil (salt it if using spaghetti). Cook the noodles per package instructions. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water, drain, and return the noodles to the pot off the heat.
  - Add the peanut butter, butter, Parmesan, and soy sauce. Stir vigorously for a minute, adding reserved cooking water a tablespoon or two at a time, until the sauce is glossy and clings to the noodles. Season with salt.
  - Top with more cheese if desired and serve immediately.
notes:
  - Amounts double or quadruple easily for more servings.
