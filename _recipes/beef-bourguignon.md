steps:
  - Season the beef with 2 tsp salt and the pepper. Let sit at room temperature at least 30 minutes, or chill up to 24 hours.
  - Cook the lardons over medium-low heat in a large Dutch oven until the fat renders and they're browned and crisp, 10–15 minutes. Transfer to a paper-towel-lined plate, keeping the fat in the pot.
  - Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Raise the heat under the pot to medium-high until the fat starts to smoke. Brown half the beef in a single layer, 10–15 minutes, transferring pieces out as they brown. Repeat with the rest.
  - Reduce heat if needed. Stir in the onion, carrot, and remaining 1/4 tsp salt, and cook until soft, about 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic and tomato paste, cook 1 minute. Stir in the flour, cook 1 minute, then add the wine, bay leaf, and thyme, scraping up the browned bits. Return the beef and half the lardons to the pot.
  - text: Cover and transfer to the oven. Cook until the beef is very tender, about 1½ hours, turning the meat halfway through.
    timer_minutes: 90
  - text: Meanwhile, combine the pearl onions, mushrooms, 1/4 cup water, the olive oil, and a pinch each of salt, pepper, and sugar in a large skillet over high heat. Bring to a simmer, cover, reduce to medium, and cook 15 minutes. Uncover, raise heat to high, and cook, tossing frequently, until well browned, 5–7 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - To serve, scatter the onions, mushrooms, and remaining lardons over the stew, then top with parsley.
notes:
  - Best made a day or two ahead — just hold off on sautéing the mushrooms and pearl onions until just before serving.
  - Use a wine you'd actually drink; it makes a real difference in the finished dish.
