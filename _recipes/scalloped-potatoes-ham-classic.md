steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Butter a baking dish.
  - Melt 2 tbsp butter in a saucepan over medium-high. Stir in the flour and cook 1 minute.
  - Remove from heat and whisk in the milk. Return to heat and bring to a simmer, stirring, until thickened. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper.
  - Cook the onions in 1 tbsp melted butter in a skillet until golden brown, and season with salt and pepper.
  - Spread 1/3 of the white sauce in the bottom of the baking dish. Top with half the potatoes, half the onions, half the ham, half the cheese, and another third of the sauce.
  - Repeat the layers, ending with the remaining cheese on top.
  - text: Bake for 45 minutes, or until golden and bubbling.
