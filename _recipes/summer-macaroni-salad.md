steps:
  - text: Cook the macaroni in well-salted water until just past al dente — a touch softer is nicer cold. Drain, rinse under cold water to stop the cooking and remove excess starch, then drain well.
  - text: While the pasta cooks, whisk together all the dressing ingredients in a large bowl until smooth. Taste and adjust — it should be a little punchy, since the pasta will mellow it.
  - text: Add the drained macaroni and all the vegetables to the bowl. Fold everything together until evenly coated.
  - text: Chill for at least 1 hour before serving. The salad absorbs dressing as it sits, so taste again before serving and loosen with an extra spoonful of mayo or a splash of vinegar if it seems dry, then re-season with salt.
