steps:
  - Set the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease the loaf pan and dust it with flour, or line it with parchment paper, leaving some overhang on the long sides as handles for lifting the loaf out later.
  - In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Scoop out about 1 tbsp into a small bowl and reserve it for tossing the raspberries.
  - Toss the raspberries with the reserved flour mixture — this keeps them from sinking to the bottom of the loaf. Set aside.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until smooth and slightly frothy, about 1–2 minutes of whisking.
  - Whisk in the oil, then the sour cream, then the vanilla extract, until the mixture is uniform.
  - Pour the wet ingredients into the bowl with the dry ingredients. Fold with a spatula until mostly combined, then stream in the hot water (or coffee) and stir until smooth. The batter will be noticeably thin — that's normal for a moist chocolate loaf.
  - Gently fold in the floured raspberries with just a few strokes, being careful not to crush them.
  - text: Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top. Bake until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean or with just a few moist crumbs, 45–55 minutes total — start checking at 45 minutes, since a full 60 runs the loaf a little dry.
    timer_minutes: 45
  - text: Let the loaf cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then turn it out onto a wire rack to cool completely before glazing with ganache — a warm loaf will just melt it right off.
    timer_minutes: 15
  - Heat the heavy cream in a small saucepan (or microwave) until just simmering. Pour over the chopped chocolate in a heatproof bowl, let sit 2 minutes, then whisk until smooth and glossy. Let it cool a few minutes until thick enough to hold its shape when drizzled, then spoon over the cooled loaf.
notes:
  - This uses the same whisk-only, no-mixer technique as the rhubarb-strawberry loaf — just eggs and sugar whisked until frothy, then the rest of the wet ingredients stirred in.
  - If using frozen raspberries, don't thaw them first — toss them frozen with the reserved flour and fold in directly, and add about 5 extra minutes to the bake time.
  - Coffee deepens the chocolate flavor without tasting like coffee itself — plain hot water works fine too.
  - Bake time reflects one test bake — worth fine-tuning further if it still runs a bit dry or underdone for your oven.
  - Store covered at room temperature 2–3 days, or in the fridge up to a week; the un-ganached loaf freezes well.
