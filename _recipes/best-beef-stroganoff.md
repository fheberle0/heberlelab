steps:
  - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  - Season the beef with 1 tsp salt and plenty of black pepper. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook untouched about 1 minute, then flip and cook until deep golden but still undercooked in the center, about 1 more minute. Transfer to a bowl.
  - Add 1 tbsp butter to the skillet with the onion and 1/2 tsp salt. Cook, scraping up the browned bits, until softened, 4–5 minutes. Add 2 tbsp butter, both mushrooms, and thyme, and cook, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms release liquid and start to crisp, 5–6 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  - Pour in the wine, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until evaporated, about 5 minutes.
  - Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir to incorporate. Fold in the sour cream, Dijon, Worcestershire, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Pour in the beef broth and stir until smooth.
  - text: Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened, about 5 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - Meanwhile, cook the egg noodles to al dente. Drain and toss with the remaining 2 tbsp butter, parsley, and chives.
  - Fold the beef and its juices into the sauce and cook, stirring, until the beef is cooked through but still slightly pink inside, about 1 minute.
  - Divide the noodles among bowls, top with the sauce and beef, and sprinkle with more chives if desired.
notes:
  - A marbled, tender cut like ribeye is worth it here — two kinds of mushrooms cooked until crispy add extra depth.
