steps:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté (normal). Add the sesame oil, then the garlic and ginger, and cook 30 seconds.
  - Add the sliced beef, season lightly with salt and pepper, and sear 2–3 minutes until lightly browned.
  - Whisk the soy sauce, brown sugar, vinegar, cornstarch, and broth until smooth. Pour over the beef and stir to coat.
  - text: Lock the lid and cook on high pressure for 10 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release the rest.
    timer_minutes: 10
  - Set the pot to sauté, stir in the green onions, and simmer 2–3 minutes to thicken the sauce slightly.
  - Serve the beef and sauce over rice, garnished with sesame seeds and extra green onions.
notes:
