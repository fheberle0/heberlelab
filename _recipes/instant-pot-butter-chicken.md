steps:
  - Place all ingredients into the Instant Pot in the order listed, except the butter, cream, and the finishing garam masala — mix the sauce well before placing the chicken on top. If frozen, push the chicken into the sauce a bit so it defrosts better.
  - text: Close the lid and cook on high pressure for 10 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then release the rest.
    timer_minutes: 10
  - Open the pot and carefully remove the chicken; set aside.
  - Blend the remaining sauce, preferably with an immersion blender.
  - Add the cubed butter, cream, cilantro, and remaining garam masala, and stir until well incorporated. (Let the sauce cool slightly first — adding these to boiling sauce makes it too thin; if that happens, refrigerate briefly to thicken.)
  - Optionally set aside half the sauce to freeze for later use.
  - Add the chicken back in and heat through, breaking it into smaller pieces if needed — don't shred it.
  - Serve over rice or zucchini noodles.
notes:
  - Garam masala is essential here — a homemade blend makes a real difference over store-bought.
  - No added water is needed — the tomatoes and chicken release plenty of liquid on their own.
  - Don't re-pressure-cook sauce that already has the butter and cream stirred in — it gets thin.
  - "Using leftover sauce: gently reheat with cooked chicken (or paneer and peas for a vegetarian version) and simmer briefly for the flavors to meld."
