steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Spray baking dishes with cooking spray.
  - Spread a thin layer of Boom-Boom sauce on the bottom of each pan.
  - Lay out the tortillas on foil or parchment.
  - Place 2 tbsp each of chicken and pepper jack on each tortilla.
  - Roll the tortillas and place seam-side down atop the sauce in the baking dish.
  - Pour Boom-Boom sauce over the tortillas, and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  - text: Cover and bake 20 minutes until warm throughout.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - text: Uncover and bake 5 more minutes if needed to finish melting the cheese.
