steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a 9x13-inch (23x33cm) baking pan.
  - Cook the ground beef in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat until browned through, breaking it into small pieces. Transfer with a slotted spoon to a bowl, reserving 1 tbsp of the grease.
  - Add the onion to the pan and cook 5 minutes in the reserved grease until soft and translucent. Stir in the green chiles and beans and cook 1 minute to warm. Remove from heat and stir in the ground beef and 1/4 cup enchilada sauce.
  - Set up an assembly line of tortillas, sauce, beef mixture, and cheese. Spread 2 tbsp sauce over each tortilla, add about 1/8 of the beef mixture down the center, sprinkle with 1/3 cup cheese, then roll and place seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
  - Spread any remaining sauce over the top and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  - text: Bake uncovered for 20 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - Serve immediately, garnished with fresh cilantro if desired.
