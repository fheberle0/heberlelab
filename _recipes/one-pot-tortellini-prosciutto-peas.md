steps:
  - Melt the butter over medium in a large nonstick skillet. Add the prosciutto in a single layer and cook, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp, 2–4 minutes. Transfer to a plate, leaving the fat in the pan.
  - Add the shallot to the skillet and cook over medium until softened, 2–4 minutes, adding a bit more butter if the pan is dry.
  - text: Add the tortellini, peas, broth, cream, and nutmeg (if using), and season with salt and pepper. Simmer over medium-high, stirring occasionally, until the pasta and peas are tender, 3–5 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 4
  - Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon zest and juice. Season to taste. Crumble the prosciutto on top and serve.
notes:
  - The sauce thickens as it cools, so don't overcook it looking for a thicker consistency in the pan.
  - Bacon works in place of prosciutto for a smokier version.
