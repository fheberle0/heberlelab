steps:
  - Chop the white onion.
  - Portion the burger meat into 125g balls, loosely packed by hand.
  - Spread a thin layer of mayo on the buns.
  - Heat the pan on high heat. Partway through heating, toast the buns.
  - When the pan is at full heat, drop two of the balls onto the hot surface and immediately smash them down flat. Salt once smashed.
  - text: After 2 minutes, flip the patties. Add a slice of American cheese and put a lid on the pan.
    timer_minutes: 2
  - text: Wait 30 seconds to 1 minute for the cheese to melt, then remove the burgers.
    timer_minutes: 1
  - Garnish with raw yellow onion, burger sauce, and mustard.
