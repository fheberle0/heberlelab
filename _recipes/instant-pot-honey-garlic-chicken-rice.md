steps:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté (normal). Add the oil, then the chicken. Season lightly with salt and pepper and sear 2 minutes. Add garlic and sauté 30 seconds.
  - Whisk the soy sauce, honey, ketchup, vinegar, broth, and ginger together. Pour into the pot and stir to coat the chicken.
  - Stir in the rinsed rice and the broth or water for the rice. Push the chicken pieces up to the top layer for even cooking.
  - text: Lock the lid, set the valve to sealing, and cook on high pressure for 6 minutes. Let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes, then quick-release the rest.
    timer_minutes: 6
  - If thickening, whisk the cornstarch with cold water, stir into the hot mixture, and simmer on sauté for 1–2 minutes.
  - Fluff the rice, stir everything together, and top with sesame seeds and green onions. Serve with steamed broccoli or carrots if desired.
