steps:
  - Heat the oil over medium in a medium saucepan. Add the onion and garlic, cover, and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the onions are soft but not browned.
  - Stir in the flour and cook 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly, until golden and toasty (it's fine if it clumps around the onions).
  - Gradually add the broth, whisking quickly and constantly to smooth out any lumps.
  - text: Add the remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until silky and thickened — able to coat a spoon but still drip off. Thin with more broth if needed.
    timer_minutes: 30
  - Add more salt to taste. Blend for a smoother consistency if preferred.
notes:
  - Keeps in the fridge up to 2 weeks, or freeze for 1–2 months if well sealed.
  - "To roast fresh chiles: char over a gas flame or under the broiler until blackened all over, then let cool in a covered bowl before peeling."
  - Canned chiles usually have added sodium — rinsing them first helps control the saltiness.
