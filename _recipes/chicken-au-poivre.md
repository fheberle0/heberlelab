steps:
  - Place the peppercorns in a small resealable bag and crush coarsely with a mallet or the bottom of a saucepan (or use a mortar and pestle). Set aside.
  - Heat the oil over medium in a 12-inch cast-iron or other heavy skillet. Season the chicken with salt. Sear in two batches until light golden all over, about 5 minutes per batch. Transfer to a plate and pour off any remaining oil.
  - Add the butter and shallot to the skillet and cook, stirring, until the butter melts and the shallot softens, 1 minute. Add the broth, cream, thyme sprigs, and cracked peppercorns, stirring to lift up any browned bits.
  - text: Add the chicken and any accumulated juices, bring to a simmer, and cook, turning and basting occasionally, until cooked through and an instant-read thermometer reads 74°C (165°F), 6–8 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 7
  - Divide the chicken among plates and discard the thyme.
  - Add the lemon juice to the skillet and stir until the sauce thickens slightly, about 2 minutes. Season with salt and stir in the parsley.
  - Spoon the sauce over the chicken, garnish with more parsley, and serve with crusty bread or egg noodles if desired.
notes:
  - No Cognac in this version, unlike classic steak au poivre — lemon juice brightens the sauce instead.
  - Leftovers are good in a sandwich roll with lettuce and tomato, or chopped and tossed with pasta and Parmesan.
