steps:
  - text: Melt the butter in a medium pot over medium heat. Carefully add 1 cup of the cream and bring to a boil over medium-high. Reduce to medium and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 5 minutes, until the cream thickens, reduces to about a third of its volume, resembles melted cheese, and develops brown bits around the pot.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - text: Add the shallots and continue stirring 4–5 minutes, until there's very little cream left and caramelized brown bits line the sides of the pot.
    timer_minutes: 4
  - Add the tomatoes and their juices, crushing them with your hands or a potato masher as you go. Add the lentils, water, and a big pinch of salt. Scrape the browned bits off the bottom and sides into the liquid.
  - text: Bring to a boil over medium-high. Partly cover and boil, stirring occasionally, for 15 minutes. Stir in the remaining 1/3 cup cream and season with more salt if needed.
    timer_minutes: 15
  - Serve right away, or blend with an immersion blender until as smooth as you like.
notes:
  - Brown butter plus lentils gives this more depth and staying power than a typical tomato soup.
  - Serve with crusty sourdough toast or a grilled cheese.
