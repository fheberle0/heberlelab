steps:
  - text: Heat 6 tbsp of the oil over medium-low in a 12-inch skillet or small Dutch oven. Add the pine nuts and stir occasionally until golden brown, about 5 minutes. Turn off heat, stir in the turmeric, season lightly, and transfer to a small bowl.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - Heat the remaining 2 tbsp oil over medium-high in the same skillet. Add the shallots and a pinch of salt, cook until just softened, about 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic, and when sizzling, stir in the beans and stock. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low.
  - Sprinkle in the cheese in a few additions, stirring vigorously, until melted into the broth and creamy. Season with salt. Turn off the heat, tear or roughly chop the basil, and stir in.
  - Serve hot, drizzled with the sizzled nut oil, a squeeze of lemon juice, more black pepper, and grated Parmesan if desired.
