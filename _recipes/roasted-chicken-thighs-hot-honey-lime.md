steps:
  - text: Heat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Season the chicken all over with salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlic powder on a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Arrange skin side up and roast until browned, 20 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - While the chicken roasts, whisk the melted butter and hot sauce together. Set aside half in a separate bowl. After 20 minutes, brush the chicken with the remaining butter mixture.
  - text: Continue roasting until golden brown and cooked through, 10–15 minutes more.
    timer_minutes: 12
  - Zest the lime, then halve it. Whisk the honey and 1 tsp lime zest into the reserved butter-hot sauce mixture, then brush over the roasted chicken and squeeze the lime juice on top. Serve immediately.
notes:
  - Different hot sauces vary a lot in heat — Tabasco runs quite spicy, while something like Louisiana hot sauce is milder.
  - Good with a cucumber salad or coleslaw on the side.
