steps:
  - Trim excess skin and fat from the chicken thighs.
  - Season with salt, pepper, paprika, and oregano on the skin side.
  - Press the sauté function. Once hot, add the oil and butter to the insert.
  - When the butter is melted and sizzling, stir in the garlic, then add the chicken thighs skin side down.
  - Brown for 2–3 minutes, until brown bits form on the bottom of the pot — this builds the gravy's flavor. Don't let it burn.
  - Flip the chicken and cook for 1 more minute, then press cancel/off.
  - Remove the chicken to a plate and set aside.
  - Add the water and scrape the brown bits from the bottom of the insert with a wooden spoon.
  - Place the trivet in the pot and arrange the chicken thighs on top, skin side up.
  - Close the lid, set the valve to sealing, and pressure cook on high for 12 minutes.
  - Let the pressure release naturally for at least 5 minutes, then release any remaining pressure.
  - Move the chicken to a plate and let it rest. Remove the trivet.
  - Whisk the cornstarch with 60ml (1/4 cup) water until smooth, then stir into the liquid at the bottom of the pot. Use the sauté function to simmer a few minutes until thickened into a gravy.
notes:
  - For crispy skin, broil the cooked thighs for 2–3 minutes after pressure cooking.
  - Letting the pressure release naturally (rather than a quick release) keeps the chicken juicier.
  - Boneless, skinless thighs work too — reduce pressure cook time to about 7 minutes.
