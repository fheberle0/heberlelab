steps:
  - text: Stir the breadcrumbs into the cream and milk and let them swell for 10 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 10
  - Soften the chopped onion in the 1 tablespoon of butter over medium heat without browning it, about 5 minutes. Let it cool.
  - Mix the soaked breadcrumbs, cooled onion, egg, salt, white pepper and allspice with the ground meat, working it just until combined. Overmixing makes the meatballs tough. Fry a small test ball and adjust the seasoning.
  - text: Chill the mixture for 15 minutes so it is easier to roll.
    timer_minutes: 15
  - Peel the potatoes and cut them into even pieces. Cover with cold salted water and bring to a boil.
  - text: Boil the potatoes until very tender, about 20 minutes. Meanwhile, roll the chilled mixture with wet hands into balls about 2.5 cm (1 inch) across, around 15–20 g each.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - text: Fry the meatballs in batches over medium heat in the butter and oil, shaking the pan often so they stay round. Cook 6–8 minutes, until browned all over and 70–72°C (160–162°F) inside. Keep the pan drippings.
    timer_minutes: 7
  - Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for a minute. Mash or rice them with the butter and warm milk until loose and creamy. Season with salt and a pinch of white pepper. Keep warm.
  - In the same pan, melt the 2 tablespoons of butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for a minute. Gradually whisk in the stock, then the cream.
  - text: Simmer the gravy for 5–8 minutes until it coats a spoon. Stir in the soy sauce and season with salt and white pepper.
    timer_minutes: 6
  - Serve the meatballs with the mashed potatoes, gravy, a spoonful of lingonberry jam, and pickled cucumber if you like.
notes:
  - Makes about 40 small meatballs. A half beef, half pork mix gives the best texture and flavor.
  - Making the gravy in the same pan keeps all the browned bits from the meatballs.
  - Use starchy potatoes (mjölig potatis) for a fluffy mash. A ricer gives the smoothest result.
  - Leftover meatballs freeze well. Freeze them cooked on a tray, then bag them.
