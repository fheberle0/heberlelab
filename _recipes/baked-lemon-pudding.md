steps:
  - Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and set a rack in the middle.
  - Place the butter in a medium mixing bowl. Gradually add the sugar as you mash the mixture with a wooden spoon until it looks like damp sand.
  - Mix the egg yolks into the sugar mixture, then beat in the flour. Add the lemon zest and juice, then whisk in the milk.
  - In a separate clean large bowl, beat the egg whites to stiff peaks. Gently fold the whites into the batter by hand.
  - text: Pour the mixture into a 5-cup (1.2 liter) ceramic or glass baking dish (or a Pyrex pie plate). Bake in the middle of the oven for about 40 minutes, until just set and the top is golden brown.
    timer_minutes: 40
  - Serve warm, dusted with confectioners' sugar, with softly whipped cream alongside.
notes:
  - For individual servings, bake in ramekins or custard cups for about 20 minutes.
  - Not a soufflé, but with a similar light, airy feel. As it bakes, it separates into distinct layers, custardy on the bottom and spongy on top.
