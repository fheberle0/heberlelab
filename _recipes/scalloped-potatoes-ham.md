steps:
  - Place a rack in the center of the oven and preheat to 190°C (375°F). Coat a 9x13-inch (23x33cm) casserole dish with nonstick spray.
  - Melt the butter over medium-low in a medium pot or high-sided skillet. Add the onion and cook 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until translucent but not browned. Stir in the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute more.
  - Turn off the heat. Stir in the milk, broth, 1 tsp of the salt, and the pepper. Set aside.
  - Peel the potatoes and slice very thinly (about 1/8-inch) with a mandoline or sharp knife. Toss in a large bowl with the flour and remaining 1/2 tsp salt.
  - Spread one-third of the potato slices in the bottom of the prepared dish. Spoon 1 cup of the milk-broth mixture over the top and sprinkle with 2/3 cup ham and one-third of the cheddar. Repeat twice more, ending with cheddar.
  - text: Cover the dish with foil (spray-side down) and bake 35 minutes, then uncover and bake 25 more minutes.
    timer_minutes: 60
  - text: Sprinkle with Parmesan and broil until golden brown and bubbly, 1–3 minutes — watch closely.
    timer_minutes: 2
  - Sprinkle with herbs. Let stand at room temperature 20–25 minutes to let the milk mixture set and thicken before serving.
notes:
  - The salt needed varies with how salty your ham and broth are — taste and adjust at the end.
