steps:
  - Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the linguine until al dente. Reserve 2 cups pasta water, then drain.
  - Dry the pot and return to low heat. Add the butter, and while it melts, grate the garlic directly into the pot. Stir until fragrant, 1–2 minutes.
  - Finely grate the lemon zest into the pot and add a few grinds of black pepper. Once sizzling, add 1 cup pasta water and the cooked pasta. Raise heat to medium-high and toss vigorously.
  - text: As the liquid evaporates, add more pasta water a few tablespoons at a time, tossing vigorously, until the pasta is glossed in a thick sauce, 1–2 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 2
  - Remove from heat, squeeze in the juice of the whole lemon, and toss. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Top with Parmesan, lemon zest, crushed red pepper, and/or parsley if desired.
notes:
  - Dresses up nicely with seared scallops, shrimp, or mushrooms.
  - Any pasta shape works if you don't have linguine on hand.
