steps:
  - text: "Dough prep: whisk the flour, salt, and yeast together in a large bowl until mixed. Stir in the water until a chunky, thick dough forms — if needed, add a few more tablespoons of water, just enough to get it barely wet throughout. It will look scrappy and loose, which is normal."
  - text: Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let it rest at room temperature for 12–18 hours. Overnight is ideal.
  - text: "Prep for baking: when ready to bake, preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Place a 5.7L (6-quart) enamel-coated cast iron Dutch oven (or similar) inside to heat for about 30 minutes."
    timer_minutes: 30
  - text: The dough should now be big, puffy, and loose, with little bubbles. Gently scrape it onto a well-floured surface — do not knead. Shape gently into a ball with flour on the outside, set on a piece of parchment, and cover with plastic while the pan finishes heating.
  - Remove the plastic. Carefully lift the dough and parchment together into the hot pan (the pan is very hot — be careful not to touch it).
  - text: Bake covered for 30 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 30
  - text: Remove the cover and bake 10–15 more minutes, until golden brown and crispy on the exterior.
