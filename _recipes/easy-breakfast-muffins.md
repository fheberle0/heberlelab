steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners or spray each cup with cooking spray.
  - In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt (plus the cinnamon, if using) and stir together.
  - Add the eggs, oil, milk, and vanilla (if using) and mix only until combined. Be careful not to overmix or the muffins will be dry. Gently fold in the mix-ins, if using.
  - text: Scoop the batter into the prepared muffin tin and bake for 15–17 minutes, or just until lightly golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Serve with butter and jam.
    timer_minutes: 16
notes:
  - The author prefers to add the 2 tsp vanilla and 1/4 tsp cinnamon for extra flavor. Her recipe change on 1/6/25 reduced the flour from 2 cups to 1 3/4 cups.
  - A large cookie scoop makes filling the tin easy and keeps the muffins even.
  - Other mix-in ideas from the author - diced strawberries, chopped peaches, raisins or dried cranberries, and chopped walnuts or pecans.
  - Storage - once cooled, keep in a sealed container for up to 3 days.
  - Freezing - freeze cooled muffins in a freezer bag or airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, or microwave one wrapped in a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds.
