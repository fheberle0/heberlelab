steps:
  - text: "Cook the rice: combine the rice, water/broth, butter, salt, and lemon zest (if using). Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and simmer 15 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork."
    timer_minutes: 15
  - "Sear the salmon: pat dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a skillet over medium-high. Sear the salmon 3–4 minutes per side until golden. Transfer to a plate and cover loosely with foil."
  - "Make the sauce: reduce heat to medium. Add the remaining butter and garlic, and cook 30 seconds. Add the lemon juice, zest, broth, and honey (if using). Simmer 2–3 minutes, scraping up browned bits. Return the salmon to the pan, spoon the sauce over, and cook 1 more minute."
  - Spoon the rice onto plates, top with salmon, drizzle with the lemon butter sauce, and garnish with parsley or dill.
notes:
  - Add 2 tbsp heavy cream at the end for a creamier sauce.
  - For a milder flavor, start with half the lemon juice and adjust at the table.
  - Good sides — steamed broccoli, peas, or roasted asparagus.
  - "Instant Pot rice alternative: 1 cup rice + 1 cup water, pressure cook 3 minutes, natural release 10 minutes."
