steps:
  - Set the Instant Pot to sauté on high. Add the butter, oil, onion, carrots, and 1 tsp salt. Sauté, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes until soft and golden.
  - Add the chicken, another 1 tsp salt, and the pepper. Sauté, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes.
  - Add the broth and rice and stir to combine. Place the garlic halves on top, cut side down.
  - text: Cover and cook on manual high pressure for 10 minutes, then quick-release the pressure.
