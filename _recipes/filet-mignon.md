steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Heat the oil in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat. Season the steaks with salt and pepper on both sides.
  - text: When the oil is just about to smoke, add the steaks. Cook until well seared, about 5 minutes, then flip and add the butter and rosemary. Baste with the butter and cook 3–5 more minutes.
    timer_minutes: 8
  - text: Transfer the skillet to the oven and cook to your liking, about 5 minutes for medium.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - Remove from the pan and let rest 5 minutes before slicing.
notes:
  - Check the internal temperature before transferring to the oven — if you're within 10°F (about 5°C) of your target, you'll need less oven time. Check every couple of minutes near the end to avoid overcooking.
