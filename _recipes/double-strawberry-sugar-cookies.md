  - section: Cookies
    text: Beat the butter, sugar, and vanilla together until combined. Beat in the egg. Add the flour, baking soda, 1/4 cup of the strawberry powder (reserve the rest for the icing), and salt. Beat in the jam.
  - section: Cookies
    text: Roll the dough into rounded tablespoon-sized balls, spaced 2 inches apart on the baking sheet.
  - section: Cookies
    text: Bake 8 minutes, then remove, rotate the pan, and tap it on the counter once to flatten the cookies. Bake 2–3 more minutes, until just beginning to set at the edges.
