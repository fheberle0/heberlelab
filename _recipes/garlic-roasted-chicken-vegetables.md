steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Remove the giblets from the chicken, season with salt and pepper, and place in a roasting pan or large cast iron skillet. Stuff the onion, rosemary, and lemon inside the chicken.
  - Combine the butter, garlic, thyme, oregano, rosemary, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Rub evenly over the outside of the chicken.
  - Toss the onion, potatoes, and carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper in a medium bowl, then arrange around the chicken in the pan.
  - text: Roast for 1 1/2 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F).
