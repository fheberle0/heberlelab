steps:
  - Whisk all the marinade ingredients together until the honey dissolves.
  - Pour about 1/4 cup (60 ml) of the marinade into a small saucepan and set it aside in the fridge. This becomes the glaze. Do not let it touch the raw chicken.
  - Put the chicken in a bag or dish, pour in the rest of the marinade, and turn to coat. Refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours. Longer than that and the citrus and soy can turn the surface mushy.
  - Heat the grill to medium-high with a hotter direct zone and a cooler indirect zone. Lightly pat the chicken dry, since the sugar burns easily.
  - text: Grill the chicken over direct heat for 5–6 minutes per side, until nicely colored.
    timer_minutes: 6
  - Move the chicken to the cooler zone, close the lid, and cook until the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F). Thighs need about 15–20 minutes in total, and boneless breasts a little less.
  - text: While the chicken finishes, simmer the reserved marinade for 2–3 minutes until slightly syrupy. Brush it on the chicken during the last few minutes of cooking.
    timer_minutes: 3
  - Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and scallions to serve.
notes:
  - Orange juice stands in for the pineapple juice found in many teriyaki-style marinades, giving similar sweetness and mild fruitiness. Keep the marinating time short, because it lacks pineapple's tenderizing enzymes.
  - For a thicker glaze, stir 1/2 tsp cornstarch into the reserved marinade before simmering.
  - A squeeze of lemon at the table adds brightness.
  - Keep the chicken over the cooler zone once it has color, since the honey and sugar scorch easily.
