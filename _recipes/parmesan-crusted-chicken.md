steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
  - Pat the chicken dry, then slice each breast in half horizontally to form 2 thin cutlets (4 total).
  - Pound the cutlets to 1/4-inch thickness with a mallet or rolling pin. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper.
  - Combine the panko, Parmesan, and parsley with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper in a large shallow bowl. Add the oil and toss until evenly coated.
  - Brush each cutlet all over with about 1 tbsp mayonnaise, then dip into the panko mixture, turning and pressing gently until fully coated.
  - text: Place the breaded chicken on the prepared pan and bake 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway through, until browned and cooked through.
    timer_minutes: 22
  - Sprinkle lightly with salt and serve hot or warm.
notes:
  - Mayonnaise stands in for the usual flour-egg-breadcrumb dredge — it helps the panko stick and keeps the chicken moist.
  - Good with a simple green salad or your favorite dipping sauce.
