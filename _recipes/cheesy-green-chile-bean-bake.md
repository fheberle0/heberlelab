steps:
  - Arrange a rack in the upper third of the oven and heat the broiler to high.
  - text: Heat the oil over medium-high in a large ovenproof skillet. Add the poblanos and cook undisturbed until browned underneath, 2–3 minutes. Season with salt and pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until crisp-tender, 2–3 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 5
  - text: Stir in the beans, salsa verde, and cilantro stems, and simmer until slightly thickened, 2–3 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 3
  - text: Turn off the heat, sprinkle the cheese evenly over the top, then broil until melted and browned in spots, 2–3 minutes.
