steps:
  - Heat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Combine the broth, oil, lemon juice, and salt on a rimmed sheet pan. Toss the potatoes in the liquid to coat, then arrange cut-sides down in an even layer. Sprinkle with oregano if using.
  - text: Roast, flipping halfway through, until fork-tender, dark brown, and crispy on top, 55–60 minutes. If not as crispy as you'd like once cooked through, run under the broiler for a few minutes.
    timer_minutes: 58
  - Sprinkle with flaky salt and black pepper as desired.
notes:
  - Roasting peeled potatoes in equal parts olive oil, lemon juice, and stock keeps the insides tender while the outsides crisp up.
  - Works with russet potatoes too, though the result will be a bit less moist.
