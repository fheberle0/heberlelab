steps:
  - Heat the oven to 190°C (375°F) with a rack in the center. Generously butter a 12-cup muffin tin.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with 150g of the sugar until well combined.
  - Whisk in the sour cream, 113g of the butter, the oil, nutmeg, and 1 tsp salt until well combined.
  - Whisk in the cocoa powder, then the baking powder and baking soda. Fold in the flour with a flexible spatula, gently scooping from the bottom and folding over the top, rotating the bowl, until just incorporated.
  - text: Divide the batter among the 12 wells and bake until puffed and a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs, 18–23 minutes.
    timer_minutes: 20
  - Combine the remaining 50g sugar, the cinnamon, and a pinch of salt in a shallow bowl.
  - Let the muffins cool in the pan 5 minutes, then transfer to a rack. Brush each warm muffin with remaining melted butter, then toss in the cinnamon sugar. Repeat with the rest.
  - Serve immediately, or store in an airtight container at room temperature up to 4 days.
notes:
  - The sugar coating gradually melts into the muffins as they sit — you can re-roll them in fresh cinnamon sugar if you want the crunch back.
