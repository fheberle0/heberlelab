How You Can Help

  • Claim a shift: find an open slot on the schedule above, type your name at the top, and click it. It's claimed instantly — no need to post in the group chat first, though letting people know is still nice.
  • Pacing: he tends to move faster than is safe for him right now — a gentle reminder to slow down goes a long way.
  • O2 tubing: keep an eye on it while he's up and moving so it doesn't become a trip hazard.
  • Just visiting counts: conversation and company are as valuable as any task.
  • Meals: home-cooked food is always welcome (a crockpot meal works great) — coordinate timing in the group chat so we don't double up.
  • Rides: if you can help with transportation to the Senior Center, church, or appointments, post in the group chat.

Daily Schedule to Keep in Mind

  • Morning medications: given after breakfast. He needs to stay upright (not lie down) for 30 minutes afterward.
  • Evening medication: given right after dinner.
  • Nebulizer treatments: 4 times a day, roughly 8am / 12pm / 4pm / 8pm.
  • If you're not sure whether something's been given already, check the group chat or ask whoever was here before you.
