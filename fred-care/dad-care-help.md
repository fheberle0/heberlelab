## How You Can Help

- **Claim a shift:** Look at the calendar above, find an open slot, and post in the group chat (e.g., "I can take Thursday afternoon"). It'll be confirmed and added.
- **What a visit usually looks like:**
  - Help him move slowly and carefully — he tends to move faster than is safe for him right now, so gentle reminders to take his time really matter.
  - If it's a mealtime, keep an eye on the O2 tubing so it doesn't become a trip hazard while he's up and moving.
  - A friendly visit and conversation is just as valuable as a "task" — he doesn't need to be busy the whole time.
- **Meals:** If you'd like to help with a meal, home-cooked food (a crockpot meal works great) is always welcome — just coordinate timing in the group chat so we don't double up.
- **Transportation:** If you're able to help with rides (to the Senior Center, church, or appointments), post in the group chat.

## Daily Schedule to Keep in Mind

- **Morning medications:** given after breakfast. He needs to stay upright (not lie down) for 30 minutes afterward.
- **Evening medication:** given right after dinner.
- **Nebulizer treatments:** 4 times a day, roughly 8am / 12pm / 4pm / 8pm.
- If you're not sure whether something's been given already, check the group chat or ask whoever was here before you.

## Care Tips

- **Pacing:** He tends to move too quickly for his current strength — a gentle "let's slow down" goes a long way toward preventing a fall.
- **Footwear:** Grip socks (non-slip on the bottom) when he's not wearing shoes.
- **O2 tubing:** Keep it clear of walking paths — tuck it along furniture/walls rather than letting it stretch across open floor.
- **Stairs:** Use the gait belt if he's navigating stairs.
- **Lighting:** Motion-sensor lights are being added around the space — if you notice a dark spot that could use one, mention it in the group chat.

## Equipment

The **Forsyth Senior Center** (contact Tim Blaisdell, above) has walkers, wheelchairs, and shower chairs available to borrow — check with them before buying anything new.
