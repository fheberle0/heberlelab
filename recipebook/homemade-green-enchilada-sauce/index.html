Homemade Green Enchilada Sauce

Mexican 48 min

Adapted from Mel's Kitchen Cafe

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp 30 ml oil
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml onion, finely chopped
  • 1-2 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 3 tbsp 24 g flour
  • 1/4 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cup 360 ml low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup 240 ml Anaheim or poblano chiles, roasted and peeled (or 1 cup canned diced green chiles, rinsed)
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 jalapeño, finely diced (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oil over medium in a medium saucepan. Add the onion and garlic, cover, and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the onions are soft but not browned.
  2. Stir in the flour and cook 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly, until golden and toasty (it's fine if it clumps around the onions).
  3. Gradually add the broth, whisking quickly and constantly to smooth out any lumps.
  4. Add the remaining ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until silky and thickened — able to coat a spoon but still drip off. Thin with more broth if needed.
  5. Add more salt to taste. Blend for a smoother consistency if preferred.

Notes

  • Keeps in the fridge up to 2 weeks, or freeze for 1–2 months if well sealed.
  • To roast fresh chiles: char over a gas flame or under the broiler until blackened all over, then let cool in a covered bowl before peeling.
  • Canned chiles usually have added sodium — rinsing them first helps control the saltiness.
