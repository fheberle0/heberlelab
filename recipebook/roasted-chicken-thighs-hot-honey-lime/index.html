Roasted Chicken Thighs with Hot Honey and Lime

Chicken 35 min

Adapted from NYT Cooking (Vallery Lomas)

Ingredients

  • 3 lb 1.4 kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • to taste salt and black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp 28 g butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml hot sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml mild honey

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 230°C (450°F). Season the chicken all over with salt, pepper, onion powder, and garlic powder on a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Arrange skin side up and roast until browned, 20 minutes.
  2. While the chicken roasts, whisk the melted butter and hot sauce together. Set aside half in a separate bowl. After 20 minutes, brush the chicken with the remaining butter mixture.
  3. Continue roasting until golden brown and cooked through, 10–15 minutes more.
  4. Zest the lime, then halve it. Whisk the honey and 1 tsp lime zest into the reserved butter-hot sauce mixture, then brush over the roasted chicken and squeeze the lime juice on top. Serve immediately.

Notes

  • Different hot sauces vary a lot in heat — Tabasco runs quite spicy, while something like Louisiana hot sauce is milder.
  • Good with a cucumber salad or coleslaw on the side.
