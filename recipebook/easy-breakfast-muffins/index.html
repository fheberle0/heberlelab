Easy Breakfast Muffins

Bread 20 min

Adapted from I Heart Naptime (Jamielyn Nye)

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cup 230 g all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled
  • 1/2 cup 98 g granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml vegetable oil
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml milk
  • 2 tsp 10 ml vanilla extract (optional, author's preference)
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (optional, author's preference)
  • 1/2 cup blueberries, chocolate chips, or cinnamon chips (optional mix-in)
  • to serve butter and jam, for serving

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners or spray each cup with cooking spray.
  2. In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt (plus the cinnamon, if using) and stir together.
  3. Add the eggs, oil, milk, and vanilla (if using) and mix only until combined. Be careful not to overmix or the muffins will be dry. Gently fold in the mix-ins, if using.
  4. Scoop the batter into the prepared muffin tin and bake for 15–17 minutes, or just until lightly golden and a toothpick comes out clean. Serve with butter and jam.

Notes

  • The author prefers to add the 2 tsp vanilla and 1/4 tsp cinnamon for extra flavor. Her recipe change on 1/6/25 reduced the flour from 2 cups to 1 3/4 cups.
  • A large cookie scoop makes filling the tin easy and keeps the muffins even.
  • Other mix-in ideas from the author - diced strawberries, chopped peaches, raisins or dried cranberries, and chopped walnuts or pecans.
  • Storage - once cooled, keep in a sealed container for up to 3 days.
  • Freezing - freeze cooled muffins in a freezer bag or airtight container for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, or microwave one wrapped in a damp paper towel for 15–30 seconds.
