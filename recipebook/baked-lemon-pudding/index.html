Baked Lemon Pudding

Desserts 60 min

Adapted from NYT Cooking (David Tanis, adapted from JR Ryall)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp 15 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 1/8 cup 225 g granulated sugar
  • 3 large eggs, yolks and whites separated
  • 3 tbsp 30 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 90 ml large lemons, grated and juiced (about 2 tbsp zest and 6 tbsp juice)
  • 1 cup 250 ml whole milk
  • to taste confectioners' sugar, for dusting
  • to serve softly whipped cream, for serving

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F) and set a rack in the middle.
  2. Place the butter in a medium mixing bowl. Gradually add the sugar as you mash the mixture with a wooden spoon until it looks like damp sand.
  3. Mix the egg yolks into the sugar mixture, then beat in the flour. Add the lemon zest and juice, then whisk in the milk.
  4. In a separate clean large bowl, beat the egg whites to stiff peaks. Gently fold the whites into the batter by hand.
  5. Pour the mixture into a 5-cup (1.2 liter) ceramic or glass baking dish (or a Pyrex pie plate). Bake in the middle of the oven for about 40 minutes, until just set and the top is golden brown.
  6. Serve warm, dusted with confectioners' sugar, with softly whipped cream alongside.

Notes

  • For individual servings, bake in ramekins or custard cups for about 20 minutes.
  • Not a soufflé, but with a similar light, airy feel. As it bakes, it separates into distinct layers, custardy on the bottom and spongy on top.
