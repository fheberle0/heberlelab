Instant Pot Chicken and Rice

Chicken 30 min

Adapted from NatashasKitchen.com

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp 30 ml olive oil
  • 2 tbsp 28 g unsalted butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 large carrots, grated or julienned
  • 1 1/2 lb 680 g boneless, skinless chicken (breast, thigh, or tenders), cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 4 cup 950 ml low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 cup 400 g jasmine rice, un-rinsed
  • 1 head garlic, unpeeled, halved crosswise
  • 1/3 cup 80 ml parsley, finely chopped, plus more to garnish
  • 1/2 cup 50 g shredded parmesan, plus more to serve

Method

  1. Set the Instant Pot to sauté on high. Add the butter, oil, onion, carrots, and 1 tsp salt. Sauté, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Add the chicken, another 1 tsp salt, and the pepper. Sauté, stirring occasionally, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the broth and rice and stir to combine. Place the garlic halves on top, cut side down.
  4. Cover and cook on manual high pressure for 10 minutes, then quick-release the pressure.
  5. Stir in the parmesan and parsley. Serve with more cheese and parsley if desired.
