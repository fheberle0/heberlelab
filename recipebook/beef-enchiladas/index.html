Beef Enchiladas

Beef 60 min

Adapted from Gimme Some Oven

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb 680 g lean ground beef
  • to taste salt and pepper
  • 1 small white onion, diced
  • 4 oz 115 g diced green chiles (4 oz can)
  • 15.5 oz 440 g black beans or lentils, rinsed and drained (15.5 oz can)
  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 3 cup 340 g Mexican-blend shredded cheese
  • 1 batch red enchilada sauce, homemade or store-bought
  • 1/4 cup 60 ml fresh cilantro, chopped (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a 9x13-inch (23x33cm) baking pan.
  2. Cook the ground beef in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat until browned through, breaking it into small pieces. Transfer with a slotted spoon to a bowl, reserving 1 tbsp of the grease.
  3. Add the onion to the pan and cook 5 minutes in the reserved grease until soft and translucent. Stir in the green chiles and beans and cook 1 minute to warm. Remove from heat and stir in the ground beef and 1/4 cup enchilada sauce.
  4. Set up an assembly line of tortillas, sauce, beef mixture, and cheese. Spread 2 tbsp sauce over each tortilla, add about 1/8 of the beef mixture down the center, sprinkle with 1/3 cup cheese, then roll and place seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
  5. Spread any remaining sauce over the top and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  6. Bake uncovered for 20 minutes.
  7. Serve immediately, garnished with fresh cilantro if desired.
