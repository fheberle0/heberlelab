Swedish Meatballs (Köttbullar) with Mashed Potatoes and Lingonberry Jam

Beef 75 min

Adapted from Claude (custom recipe)

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 1/2 cup 100 ml breadcrumbs
  • scant 1/2 cup 100 ml heavy cream
  • 3 1/2 tbsp 50 ml milk
  • 1 small yellow onion, very finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp 14 g butter, for the onion
  • 1 lb 500 g ground meat, ideally half beef and half pork
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 2 tbsp 28 g butter, for frying
  • 1 tbsp 15 ml neutral oil, for frying

Cream gravy

  • 2 tbsp 28 g butter
  • 2 tbsp 16 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/4 cup 300 ml beef stock
  • 3/4 cup 200 ml heavy cream
  • 1 tsp 5 ml soy sauce, for color and depth
  • to taste salt and white pepper

Mashed potatoes

  • 1 3/4 lb 800 g starchy potatoes (mjölig potatis), peeled
  • 3 1/2 tbsp 50 g butter
  • 2/3 to 3/4 cup 150-200 ml warm milk
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, plus a pinch of white pepper

To serve

  • to serve lingonberry jam
  • to serve pickled cucumber (optional)

Method

  1. Stir the breadcrumbs into the cream and milk and let them swell for 10 minutes.
  2. Soften the chopped onion in the 1 tablespoon of butter over medium heat without browning it, about 5 minutes. Let it cool.
  3. Mix the soaked breadcrumbs, cooled onion, egg, salt, white pepper and allspice with the ground meat, working it just until combined. Overmixing makes the meatballs tough. Fry a small test ball and adjust the seasoning.
  4. Chill the mixture for 15 minutes so it is easier to roll.
  5. Peel the potatoes and cut them into even pieces. Cover with cold salted water and bring to a boil.
  6. Boil the potatoes until very tender, about 20 minutes. Meanwhile, roll the chilled mixture with wet hands into balls about 2.5 cm (1 inch) across, around 15–20 g each.
  7. Fry the meatballs in batches over medium heat in the butter and oil, shaking the pan often so they stay round. Cook 6–8 minutes, until browned all over and 70–72°C (160–162°F) inside. Keep the pan drippings.
  8. Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for a minute. Mash or rice them with the butter and warm milk until loose and creamy. Season with salt and a pinch of white pepper. Keep warm.
  9. In the same pan, melt the 2 tablespoons of butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for a minute. Gradually whisk in the stock, then the cream.
  10. Simmer the gravy for 5–8 minutes until it coats a spoon. Stir in the soy sauce and season with salt and white pepper.
  11. Serve the meatballs with the mashed potatoes, gravy, a spoonful of lingonberry jam, and pickled cucumber if you like.

Notes

  • Makes about 40 small meatballs. A half beef, half pork mix gives the best texture and flavor.
  • Making the gravy in the same pan keeps all the browned bits from the meatballs.
  • Use starchy potatoes (mjölig potatis) for a fluffy mash. A ricer gives the smoothest result.
  • Leftover meatballs freeze well. Freeze them cooked on a tray, then bag them.
