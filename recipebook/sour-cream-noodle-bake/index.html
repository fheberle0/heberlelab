Sour Cream Noodle Bake

Beef 30 min

Adapted from Food Network (Ree Drummond)

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 lb 570 g ground chuck
  • 15 oz 425 g tomato sauce (one 15 oz can)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • to taste black pepper
  • 8 oz 227 g egg noodles
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml sour cream
  • 1 1/4 cup 280 g small curd cottage cheese
  • pinch red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml green onions, sliced
  • 1 cup 100 g sharp Cheddar, grated
  • to taste crusty bread, for serving

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F).
  2. Brown the ground chuck in a large skillet. Drain the fat, then add the tomato sauce, salt, and plenty of black pepper. Simmer while you prepare the other ingredients.
  3. Cook the egg noodles until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  4. Combine the sour cream and cottage cheese in a medium bowl with plenty of black pepper and a pinch of red pepper flakes. Stir into the noodles, then add the green onions.
  5. Layer half the noodles in a baking dish, top with half the meat mixture, then half the Cheddar. Repeat with the remaining noodles, meat, and a final layer of cheese.
  6. Bake until all the cheese is melted, about 20 minutes.
  7. Serve with crusty bread.

Notes

  • To freeze: assemble in a disposable oven-proof pan, seal tightly, and freeze.
  • To cook from frozen: bake covered at 190°C (375°F) for 45 minutes, then uncover and bake until lightly browned and bubbly, about 20 more minutes.
