Classic Scalloped Potatoes

Potatoes 75 min

Adapted from The Girl Who Ate Everything (Christy Denney)

Ingredients

  • 4 cup 900 g potatoes (Yukon Gold or Russet), thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp 42 g butter
  • 3 tbsp 24 g all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cup 360 ml milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 1/2 cup 170 g sharp cheddar cheese, grated, divided
  • to taste paprika, for color

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease a 1.5-quart (about 1.4L) baking dish.
  2. Melt the butter in a small saucepan and stir in the flour. Whisk in the milk slowly, and season with salt and cayenne.
  3. Cook the sauce over low heat until smooth and boiling, stirring occasionally.
  4. Reduce heat and stir in 1 cup of the cheddar.
  5. Layer half the sliced potatoes in the baking dish, then pour half the cheese sauce over them. Repeat with the remaining potatoes and sauce.
  6. Sprinkle the remaining cheddar on top, and dust with paprika.
  7. Bake uncovered about 1 hour, or until the potatoes are fork-tender.

Notes

  • Slice the potatoes thin — this is the single most important step for good texture.
  • Yukon Gold and Russet potatoes both work well here.
