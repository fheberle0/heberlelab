Minty Pea Risotto

Rice 26 min

Adapted from Instant Pot Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp 57 g unsalted butter
  • 2 medium shallots, peeled and diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
  • 1 1/2 cup 300 g Arborio rice
  • 4 cup 960 ml chicken broth, divided
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • 3/4 cup 20 g fresh mint leaves, chopped
  • 1 cup 150 g fresh or frozen peas

Method

  1. Press the Sauté button on the Instant Pot. Add the butter and heat 30 seconds until melted. Add the shallots and stir-fry 5 minutes until translucent. Add the garlic and rice and cook for an additional minute.
  2. Add 1 cup of the broth and stir 3 minutes until it is absorbed by the rice. Add the remaining 3 cups broth, the salt, and the pepper. Lock the lid.
  3. Press Manual or Pressure Cook and set the time to 10 minutes.
  4. When the timer beeps, let the pressure release naturally for 10 minutes. Quick-release any remaining pressure until the float valve drops, then unlock the lid.
  5. Stir in the lime zest, mint, and peas. Heat 3 minutes until the peas are heated through.
  6. Ladle into four bowls and serve warm.

Notes

  • Mint and lamb go together beautifully, so this is a natural side for lamb chops. It also pairs well with shrimp, fish, or a juicy steak.
