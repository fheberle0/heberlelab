Banana Pancakes

Breakfast 25 min

Adapted from Once Upon a Chef (Jennifer Segal)

Ingredients

Pancakes

  • 1 1/2 cup 190 g all-purpose flour, spooned into measuring cup and leveled off
  • 2 tbsp 25 g sugar
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 small over-ripe banana, peeled (the browner, the better)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup plus 2 tbsp 270 ml milk
  • 1/2 tsp 2.5 ml vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp 43 g unsalted butter, melted

Cooking

  • 1 to 2 tbsp 15-30 ml vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp 14 g unsalted butter

Serving

  • to serve maple syrup
  • to serve sliced bananas

Method

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
  2. In a small bowl, mash the banana with a fork until almost smooth. Whisk in the eggs, then add the milk and vanilla and whisk until well blended.
  3. Pour the banana mixture and the melted butter into the flour mixture. Fold the batter gently with a rubber spatula until just blended; do not over-mix. The batter will be thick and lumpy.
  4. Set a griddle or non-stick pan over medium heat until hot. Put a pad of butter and 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil onto the griddle and swirl it around.
  5. Drop the batter by 1/4-cupfuls (60 ml) onto the griddle, spacing the pancakes about 2 inches (5 cm) apart. Cook until a few holes form on top of each pancake and the underside is golden brown, about 2 minutes.
  6. Flip the pancakes and cook until the bottom is golden brown and the top is puffed, 1 to 2 minutes more. Transfer to a serving plate.
  7. Wipe the griddle clean with paper towels, add more butter and oil, and repeat with the remaining batter. Serve hot with maple syrup and sliced bananas, if desired.

Notes

  • Makes about 12 pancakes. The source's nutrition information is given per serving for 6 servings (2 pancakes each).
  • Adapted by the source from the Williams-Sonoma Kid's Cookbook by Abigail Johnson Dodge.
