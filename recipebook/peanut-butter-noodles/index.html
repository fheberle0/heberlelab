Peanut Butter Noodles

Pasta 20 min

Adapted from NYT Cooking (Eric Kim)

Ingredients

  • to taste salt
  • 4 oz 115 g spaghetti or 1 package instant ramen (seasoning saved for later)
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml creamy peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp 14 g unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp 15 ml finely grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 1 tsp soy sauce

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil (salt it if using spaghetti). Cook the noodles per package instructions. Reserve 1/2 cup of the cooking water, drain, and return the noodles to the pot off the heat.
  2. Add the peanut butter, butter, Parmesan, and soy sauce. Stir vigorously for a minute, adding reserved cooking water a tablespoon or two at a time, until the sauce is glossy and clings to the noodles. Season with salt.
  3. Top with more cheese if desired and serve immediately.

Notes

  • Amounts double or quadruple easily for more servings.
  • For a vegan version, swap the butter for olive oil and the Parmesan for nutritional yeast.
