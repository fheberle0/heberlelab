Olive Garden Chicken Scampi Pasta

Chicken 45 min

Adapted from Dinner, then Dessert (Sabrina Snyder)

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp 56 g unsalted butter, divided
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 yellow bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml garlic, minced
  • 4 tbsp 32 g flour, divided
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 cup 240 ml white wine
  • 1 cup 240 ml chicken broth
  • 1 lb 450 g angel hair pasta
  • 1 lb 450 g chicken breast tenderloins
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp coarse ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml olive oil
  • 1/2 cup 120 ml heavy cream, warmed
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 cup 100 g parmesan cheese, shaved, divided

Method

  1. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a cast iron skillet over medium-high heat and cook the bell peppers and onion 2–3 minutes until slightly softened. Remove and set aside.
  2. Add the remaining 2 tbsp butter to the pan. Once melted, whisk in 2 tbsp of the flour and the garlic, cooking about 20 seconds.
  3. Add the red pepper flakes, oregano, basil, wine, and chicken broth. Let reduce over medium heat about 18–20 minutes.
  4. While the sauce reduces, cook the pasta one minute short of the package instructions. Do not rinse.
  5. Heat the olive oil in a second pan over medium-high heat. Coat the chicken tenderloins in salt and pepper, then toss in the remaining flour. Cook 3–4 minutes per side, then remove from heat.
  6. Once the sauce has reduced by half, whisk in the heavy cream until thickened. Stir in the lemon juice and half the parmesan.
  7. Toss the pasta with the sauce, bell peppers, and onion. Top with the chicken and remaining parmesan, and serve.
