Grilled Chicken Marinade

Chicken 21 min

Adapted from Carlsbad Cravings

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb 680 g boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs, pounded to even thickness
  • 1/3 cup 80 ml olive oil
  • 3 tbsp 45 ml lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp 30 ml balsamic vinegar
  • 2 tbsp 27 g brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp 15 ml Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes

Method

  1. Whisk the marinade ingredients together in a freezer bag or glass bowl. Add the chicken and turn to coat. Marinate 30 minutes at room temperature, or refrigerate up to 12 hours (longer is better, up to 4 hours is a good minimum).
  2. Remove the chicken from the fridge and let it rest 15–30 minutes before cooking.
  3. To grill: preheat to 200°C (400°F), clean and grease the grates.
  4. Grill undisturbed for 5–7 minutes per side, until the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F). Let rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Notes

  • Stovetop alternative: sear in 1 tbsp oil in a hot skillet, 3–4 minutes undisturbed, flip, cover, reduce heat to medium, and cook 5–7 more minutes to 74°C (165°F).
  • Oven alternative: bake at 220°C (425°F) uncovered for 16–18 minutes (thickness-dependent) to 74°C (165°F).
  • Works with any cut — thighs, tenderloins, drumsticks, wings — just adjust cooking time and check with a thermometer.
  • Pound chicken to even thickness first so it cooks evenly; slice very thick breasts into cutlets before marinating.
  • Don't marinate longer than about 12 hours — the acid can start to toughen the texture.
