Chicka-Chicka Boom-Boom Enchiladas

Chicken 75 min

Adapted from Family Savvy

Ingredients

  • 2 cup 280 g cooked chicken, chopped
  • 2 pint 950 ml Boom-Boom sauce (from Chuy's)
  • 2 cup 227 g pepper Jack cheese, shredded
  • 2 cup 227 g Mexican blend cheese, shredded
  • 1 cup 240 ml tomatillo salsa (green or hatch chili salsa)
  • 12-14 tortillas, corn or corn/flour blend

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Spray baking dishes with cooking spray.
  2. Spread a thin layer of Boom-Boom sauce on the bottom of each pan.
  3. Lay out the tortillas on foil or parchment.
  4. Place 2 tbsp each of chicken and pepper jack on each tortilla.
  5. Roll the tortillas and place seam-side down atop the sauce in the baking dish.
  6. Pour Boom-Boom sauce over the tortillas, and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
  7. Cover and bake 20 minutes until warm throughout.
  8. Uncover and bake 5 more minutes if needed to finish melting the cheese.

Notes

  • "Boom-Boom sauce" is a specialty sauce from the Chuy's restaurant chain (US) and may not be available outside the US — try the Boom-Boom Sauce (Copycat) recipe on this site as a substitute.
