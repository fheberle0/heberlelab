Fluffy Stovetop Rice

Rice 27 min

Adapted from (family collection)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup 200 g jasmine or basmati rice
  • 1 1/2 cup 360 ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt (optional)
  • 1 tsp neutral oil or butter (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the rice in a fine-mesh strainer or bowl under cold water, swirling with your hand, until the water runs mostly clear (about 4–5 rinses).
  2. Combine the rinsed rice, water, salt, and oil (if using) in a medium saucepan. Stir once to ensure the rice is submerged.
  3. Set over medium-high heat, uncovered, until the water begins to boil. Stir once, reduce heat to low, and cover tightly.
  4. Simmer on low for 12 minutes without lifting the lid or stirring.
  5. Remove from heat and let the pot sit, covered, for 10 minutes to let the steam redistribute.
  6. Fluff gently with a fork and let stand uncovered 2–3 minutes before serving.

Notes

  • Use a heavy-bottomed saucepan with a tight-fitting lid to prevent scorching, and avoid stirring during cooking to keep the grains from getting sticky.
  • When doubling, use about 1 1/4 cups water per cup of rice rather than a full double.
  • For a nutty aroma, stir in 1/2 tsp sesame oil after fluffing — pairs especially well with the Korean-style beef.
