Red Enchilada Sauce

Mexican 20 min

Adapted from Gimme Some Oven (Ali)

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp 30 ml avocado or olive oil
  • 2 tbsp 16 g all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup 30 g American-style chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/4 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 cup 480 ml chicken or vegetable stock
  • to taste fine sea salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a small saucepan over medium-high. Add the flour and cook 1 minute, whisking constantly.
  2. Add the chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, and oregano, and cook 1 more minute, whisking constantly.
  3. Gradually whisk in the stock until no lumps remain. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer for 10–15 minutes, uncovered, until the sauce has slightly thickened.
  4. Taste and season with salt as needed.
  5. Use immediately in your favorite recipe, or store.

Notes

  • Chili powder note: this calls for traditional American-style chili powder — a mild spice blend, not the same as international chili powders (which are often much spicier).
  • Gluten-free option: use a 1:1 gluten-free flour blend, or thicken with a cornstarch slurry instead.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days. Doesn't freeze well — it separates once thawed.
